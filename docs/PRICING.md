The live `/api/catalog` response is authoritative. Invoice creation, HTTP 402 responses, previews, and the web purchase interface all consume the same private product registry.

## `api-error-fixes`: 2,000 sats

The production price for `api-error-fixes` is deliberately **2,000 satoshis**.

It began at 10 sats, but real payment attempts to the newly established receiver failed at 10 and 500 sats. Small Lightning payments to new or private receivers can encounter practical routing and LSP fee floors in roughly the 1,000–2,000 sat range, depending on inbound liquidity, private-channel hints, base routing fees, proportional fees, and sender fee budgets.

On 2026-08-19, Phoenix successfully paid a 2,000-sat invoice generated by the live Worker. Alby credited 2,000 sats and the payment proof redeemed successfully for exactly 50 records. That successful end-to-end observation makes 2,000 sats the proven operational price for this product.

Do not reduce this product to 10 sats merely because `PRICE_SATS` or older documentation still shows that historical default.

All entry products use the same proven 2,000-sat receiving floor. A future reduction should require a documented live receiving-path test showing that smaller invoices route reliably; it must not be based on speculative trial payments.
